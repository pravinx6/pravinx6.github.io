01 — About

I write because I am curious.

Writing gives me a way to explore ideas, understand different perspectives and turn thoughts into something meaningful.

I enjoy writing about different subjects — from technology and research to student experiences, human behaviour, everyday observations and ideas that make me curious.

My goal is simple: to create writing that is clear, thoughtful and easy to connect with.

I am also continuously learning and improving my writing by researching, writing, editing and experimenting with different ways of communicating an idea.
